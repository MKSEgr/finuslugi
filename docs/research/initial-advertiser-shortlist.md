## Current ranking logic

The first written outreach should prioritize:

1. Альфа-Лизинг;
2. Реалист Банк;
3. Европлан;
4. CARCADE;
5. Балтийский лизинг.

This order is provisional. Selection is based not on nominal payout but on:

- permission for embedded partner distribution;
- traceable statuses;
- predictable cash event;
- clear deduplication;
- advertiser SLA;
- ability to reconcile each result;
- fit with the chosen equipment segment.

## Required written questions

1. Can Finuslugi use a proprietary or co-branded landing page?
2. Can professional equipment sellers and consultants be connected as distribution partners?
3. Is this treated as subagency, referral, advertising services or another model?
4. Is paid non-brand search permitted? Which exact restrictions apply?
5. Is brand bidding prohibited?
6. What event creates the right to remuneration?
7. What is the dedup window and existing-client definition?
8. Which statuses and rejection reasons are available?
9. Is API, webhook, postback or regular status export available?
10. What is the payment calendar and correction period?
11. What happens to in-flight deals after contract termination?
12. Who is responsible for advertising marking/ERIR in each traffic scenario?
13. What consent text and data-transfer route are required?
14. Can one rejected/expired lead be offered to another lessor, and after what event?
15. Are individual partner identifiers preserved through the advertiser process?

## Current conclusion

There is enough public evidence to continue Phase 0, but not enough evidence to launch traffic. Public payout numbers remain marketing signals until the rights, status model, deduplication and cash event are confirmed contractually.
