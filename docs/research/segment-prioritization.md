# First-Segment Prioritization

Date: 2026-08-23

## Decision status

`WORKING HYPOTHESIS — requires partner interviews`

The first design-partner search should prioritize **equipment sellers**, not heavy-truck dealers.

Top interview segment:

> Warehouse, packaging and selected production-equipment sellers serving SMEs.

This is not yet the final market choice. It is the most defensible starting hypothesis for R002.

## Current market evidence

### 2025 resilience

Expert RA, using Fedresurs statistics, reported that in 2025:

- new leasing agreements fell 30.3% overall;
- transport agreements fell 37%;
- special-equipment agreements fell 46%;
- the broad equipment category fell only 5%.

Source:

- [Expert RA — leasing market in 2025](https://raexpert.ru/researches/leasing/market_2025/)

### H1 2026 recovery

Expert RA reported that in H1 2026:

- the number of new leasing agreements for equipment grew 40% year over year;
- transport agreements grew only 1%;
- transferred equipment units grew 8%;
- medical, pump, telecommunications and retail equipment contributed to equipment growth.

Source:

- [Expert RA — leasing market in H1 2026](https://raexpert.ru/researches/leasing/1h_2026/)

### Heavy-truck risk

Expert RA reported a 63% decline in the heavy-truck leasing segment in 2025 and linked it to a structural crisis in freight transportation. The agency expects potential recovery in 2026, but that scenario depends on deferred demand, fleet wear, subsidies and broader transport economics.

Source:

- [Expert RA — leasing market outlook 2026](https://raexpert.ru/researches/leasing/2026/)

## Why warehouse and packaging equipment is the first interview hypothesis

### Strategic fit

- B2B-only purchase context;
- financing can directly unblock the partner's main sale;
- sellers know the asset, price and buyer intent before the finance lead is created;
- a large share of customers can be SMEs;
- equipment can have enough value to support meaningful remuneration;
- the process is less tied to the freight-cycle collapse than heavy trucks;
- the user's existing marketplace, warehouse and supplier knowledge can improve access and interview quality.

### Embedded workflow fit

A seller can create a financing path at a high-intent point:

- quotation;
- product page for high-value equipment;
- sales-manager follow-up;
- abandoned quotation due to budget;
- replacement or capacity-expansion project.

### Data advantage

The platform can capture structured context unavailable in generic search traffic:

- equipment category and model;
- seller quotation amount;
- new/used state;
- delivery date;
- required advance payment;
- client industry;
- purchase purpose;
- urgency;
- existing seller relationship.

## Why this is not a final answer

Public market growth does not prove that a specific seller will provide enough qualified demand or accept embedded distribution. The decision must be made from:

- actual monthly quotes and sales;
- financing-request frequency;
- loss reasons;
- current referral process;
- access to the decision maker;
- willingness to run real assisted leads;
- advertiser acceptance by asset class;
- time to funded outcome;
- expected cash contribution.

## Required interviews

Minimum:

- 5 warehouse/packaging-equipment sellers;
- 3 production-equipment sellers;
- 2 comparison interviews in one alternative segment.

## Go criteria

Select the segment only if at least two design partners confirm all of the following:

- recurring relevant B2B quotations;
- identifiable financing demand or lost deals caused by budget constraints;
- willingness to embed a co-branded path;
- access to real test clients;
- no need to expose the client to multiple unsolicited advertisers;
- a process owner who will supply status feedback.

## Stop criteria

Reject the segment if:

- financing is requested too rarely;
- average ticket cannot sustain the acquisition and operating cost;
- sellers insist on opaque cash payments or unsupported promises;
- advertisers do not finance the dominant equipment types;
- the seller refuses source/status tracking;
- the only growth path is generic paid-search arbitrage.
