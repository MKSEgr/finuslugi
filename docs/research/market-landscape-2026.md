# Market Landscape — August 2026

## Status

Public-source reconnaissance. Commercial terms still require written confirmation from each advertiser.

## 1. Generic financial affiliate distribution is already crowded

Publicly visible competitors include:

- [RKO-Партнёр](https://rko-partner.com/) — platform for agents, brokers and webmasters with status tracking and payment documents;
- [Пампаду](https://pampadu.ru/affiliate-program/rko/) — financial and insurance offers, including RKO;
- [RKO Group](https://rko-group.ru/landing-v2) — platform for agents, webmasters and accountants with many bank relationships;
- [Saleads](https://saleads.pro/offers/offery-partnerskie-programmy-rko) — broad CPA catalogue including RKO and business-service offers;
- [T-Партнёры для CPA-сетей](https://partners.tbank.ru/cpa/) — direct offer supply for CPA networks;
- [Admitad](https://www.admitad.ru/) — broad affiliate ecosystem.

### Implication

A new platform cannot win sustainably through:

- a larger catalogue alone;
- another generic partner cabinet;
- a higher advertised rate without better economics;
- mass onboarding of inactive webmasters;
- generic articles about banking products.

## 2. Direct advertisers already provide strong primitives

### Точка Банк

[Official partner page](https://tochka.com/partners/) publicly states:

- fixed rewards up to 20,000 RUB per client;
- percentage-based reward options;
- recurring rewards for some connected services;
- referral links;
- an API token for integration;
- eligibility for legal entities and individual entrepreneurs;
- a separate reward for recommending another partner.

### Альфа-Лизинг

[Official affiliate page](https://alfaleasing.ru/affiliate-program/) publicly states:

- an average reward reference of 100,000 RUB;
- up to 2% per completed transaction;
- partner cabinet and status tracking;
- payment after the leased asset is transferred to the client.

### ROWI

[Official agent page](https://rowi.com/agents/) publicly states:

- monthly remuneration;
- partner cabinet;
- products including factoring, guarantees and contract financing;
- onboarding for legal entities or individual entrepreneurs.

### Implication

The opportunity is not to reproduce what direct programmes already offer. Finuslugi must add value across advertisers and distribution partners:

- one normalized data model;
- one embedded partner workflow;
- cross-advertiser status normalization;
- outcome-based routing;
- unified quality analytics;
- transparent partner accruals;
- product matching based on real commercial context.

## 3. AI content volume is not a durable moat

### Yandex

Yandex Webmaster guidance states that uniqueness alone does not guarantee value and explicitly notes that AI-generated rewriting without additional value is not useful. Yandex recommends meaningful originality, accumulated expertise and information that solves a user task.

Sources:

- [Ranking and Alice AI guidance](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/epos)
- [Low-value content](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/threat/useless-content)

### Google

Google Search documentation states that generating many pages without adding user value may violate scaled-content-abuse policies. Its guidance emphasizes helpful, reliable, people-first content.

Sources:

- [Guidance on generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)
- [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- [Helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

### Implication

Finuslugi content must be downstream of proprietary operations:

- real approval and payout cohorts;
- real SLA observations;
- decision calculators;
- asset-specific eligibility patterns;
- explainable reasons for a route or rejection;
- expert commentary tied to a concrete client decision.

AI should compress analysis and operations, not manufacture page count.

## 4. Compliance is part of the product

Russian internet advertising is recorded through ERIR/ORD infrastructure. Personal-data collection and transfer require an explicit, traceable legal basis and evidence.

Public starting point:

- [Roskomnadzor internet advertising FAQ](https://rkn.gov.ru/treatments/chasto-zadavaemye-voprosy/p1575/)
- [Unified Register of Internet Advertising](https://rkn.gov.ru/activity/register-ord/unified-register-of-online-advertising/)

### Implication

Compliance cannot be added after scaling. The MVP must already version:

- consent text;
- recipient list or recipient-list version;
- time and channel of consent;
- source attribution;
- advertiser submission;
- offer terms;
- status and financial events.

## 5. Strategic choice

The first wedge is not a generic RKO comparison site.

It is:

> **Embedded finance distribution for commercial-equipment sellers and other B2B professionals already present at the moment of purchase.**

Finuslugi will initially optimize for:

- accepted and funded outcomes;
- partner-assisted commercial context;
- advertiser response speed;
- actual cash received;
- partner earnings per real core-business customer.

## 6. Durable moat hypothesis

The moat should compound through five assets:

1. **Distribution integrations** — co-branded links, forms and workflows embedded in partner sales.
2. **Outcome data** — real statuses, reasons, time-to-cash and cash realization by cohort.
3. **Routing intelligence** — deterministic first, statistical later.
4. **Operational trust** — explainable attribution and payments.
5. **Advertiser interoperability** — normalized adapters and reconciliation across programmes.

## 7. Open questions requiring direct confirmation

For every candidate advertiser:

- Is a proprietary landing page allowed?
- Are paid-search and other planned sources allowed?
- Are professional distribution partners or subagents allowed?
- Can one lead be routed to a reserve advertiser, and under what condition?
- What is the exact deduplication window?
- Which statuses are available and how fresh are they?
- Is API/postback available?
- What event creates the right to remuneration?
- What is the hold and payment schedule?
- What happens to pipeline leads after termination?
- Who is responsible for advertising marking and reporting?
- What consent language and data-transfer mechanism are required?

Until these questions are answered in writing, advertised payout figures are treated as hypotheses, not unit economics.
