# Advertiser Qualification Outreach

## Purpose

Obtain written evidence for Gate 0 before collecting traffic or building partner-facing functionality.

The objective is not to negotiate the maximum nominal payout first. It is to confirm that the proposed embedded-distribution model is permitted, traceable and economically reconcilable.

## Initial positioning

Finuslugi is developing a controlled B2B distribution channel for financial products embedded into the sales processes of commercial-equipment sellers.

Initial model:

- legal-entity/IP clients;
- high-intent equipment-purchase context;
- co-branded or proprietary client form;
- explicit consent and source attribution;
- manual qualification during pilot;
- no brand bidding without permission;
- no mass resale of raw contacts;
- no payment to downstream partners before advertiser cash is received;
- limited design-partner cohort before scale.

## Short contact message

Subject: Partnership model for embedded leasing leads from equipment sellers

Hello.

We are preparing a controlled pilot for attracting legal entities and individual entrepreneurs who are purchasing commercial equipment. The application is created inside the seller's sales process, contains the asset and quotation context, and is qualified before submission.

Before launching any traffic, we would like to confirm whether your partner programme supports this model and obtain the exact contractual and technical rules.

Please connect us with the employee responsible for agency/partner distribution or leasing brokers. We can provide the proposed client journey, source-control model and pilot limits.

## Detailed qualification message

Subject: Written qualification of an embedded partner-distribution model

Hello.

Finuslugi is developing an embedded B2B-finance distribution channel for commercial-equipment sellers. The seller does not make financing promises and does not collect sensitive documents. The client independently confirms the contact, consent and transfer of the application; the financial organisation makes the final decision and conducts the transaction.

The first pilot is intentionally limited to one equipment category and several design partners. We plan to preserve partner/source identifiers, manually qualify applications and reconcile every status through to the cash event.

Please confirm the following points in writing or provide the relevant agreement/rules:

1. Can we use a proprietary or co-branded landing page and application form?
2. Can commercial-equipment sellers and other B2B professionals participate as our distribution partners?
3. Is this permitted as subagency, referral, lead-generation/marketing services or another legal model?
4. Which traffic sources are permitted? Is non-brand paid search allowed?
5. Are brand queries and brand-containing domains prohibited?
6. What event creates the right to remuneration?
7. What is the deduplication window, and how is an existing client defined?
8. Which statuses and rejection reasons can be received?
9. Are API, webhook/postback or regular status exports available?
10. What are the hold, correction period, act and payment schedule?
11. How are in-flight applications treated after agreement termination?
12. Who is responsible for advertising marking, ORD/ERIR reporting and related payments in each traffic scenario?
13. What consent wording and data-transfer method are required?
14. Can a formally rejected or expired application be routed to a reserve lessor? Under which event and waiting period?
15. Can the original distribution-partner identifier be preserved in your process and reconciliation report?
16. Which equipment categories, ticket ranges, client ages and regions are currently accepted?
17. What SLA applies to the first client contact and status update?
18. Are pilot caps or quality thresholds required?

No advertising or partner onboarding will begin before the source and distribution model are approved.

## Call agenda — 25 minutes

### 0–3 minutes — context

- selected equipment segment;
- expected lead context;
- pilot volume;
- client and partner roles.

### 3–10 minutes — commercial event

- target action;
- payout formula;
- cash event;
- payment period;
- corrections;
- examples of common reject reasons.

### 10–17 minutes — distribution rights

- proprietary landing;
- sources;
- downstream professional partners;
- brand rules;
- exclusive/non-exclusive relationship;
- reserve routing.

### 17–22 minutes — data and integration

- fields;
- consents;
- deduplication;
- API/export;
- status freshness;
- reconciliation.

### 22–25 minutes — pilot decision

- designated owner;
- written confirmation;
- required documents;
- test cap;
- next checkpoint.

## Evidence discipline

For every material answer store privately:

- date;
- respondent and role;
- channel;
- exact wording or source document;
- applicable legal entity;
- validity period;
- unresolved contradictions.

Do not publish private terms, personal contacts or contracts in this public repository.
